# The stack

The tools these skills run alongside at 64stories. The skills themselves are
tool-agnostic: every one accepts pasted or exported data, so swap in your
equivalents freely. This list exists because "what do you actually use?" is
the most common question, and the honest answer beats a category lecture.

| Job | Tool | Where it meets the skills |
|---|---|---|
| Internal meeting capture | [Granola](https://granola.ai) | Transcripts feed `transcript-ideas`, `meeting-to-post`, `one-recording-everything`, `cross-transcript-synthesis` |
| External and sales call capture | [Sybill](https://sybill.ai) and [Gong](https://gong.io) | Same transcript path; customer language feeds `voice-pack` lexicons and `press-quotes` proof |
| Memory and notes | [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md) | A natural home for your `my-story/` pack and the idea bank; plain markdown, local, versionable |
| Tasks | [Todoist](https://todoist.com) | The daily loops (`comment-strategy` queue, `meeting-to-post` reps, weekly `what-worked` review) live as recurring tasks |
| CRM | [HubSpot](https://hubspot.com) | Where `engagement-to-pipeline` warm lists and `connections-icp-match` A-tiers land |
| Social signals and scraping | [Trigify](https://trigify.io) | Engagement and intent signals feed `engagement-to-pipeline` and `comment-strategy` |
| API access | [RapidAPI](https://rapidapi.com) | Hosted APIs for platform data the free public endpoints do not cover |
| Channels | LinkedIn first, plus other socials | Where everything ships; `platforms/` holds the per-channel constraints |
| Motion direction | [Fable](https://www.fable.app) | Storyboards, motion references, and frozen visual exports feed `fable-hyperframes-launch-video` |
| Video assembly | HyperFrames | Deterministic composition, captions, audio sync, QA, preview, and rendering for `fable-hyperframes-launch-video` |

Two rules we hold about tools:

1. The tool never does the judgment. Scoring, voice, and taste live in the
   skills and your `my-story/` pack; tools move data.
2. Nothing here is sponsored or affiliated. It is the working stack, listed
   because hiding it would be silly.
