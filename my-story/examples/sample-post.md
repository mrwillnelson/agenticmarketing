$212,000 of our cash was sitting in other people's accounts payable queues.

I found out on a Tuesday, from the aging report, not from our dashboard.
The dashboard said we'd just had our best quarter. The aging report said
14 of our 38 open invoices were more than 45 days late.

We had no dunning process. I was the dunning process, and I'd been busy.

It took six weeks to pull DSO from 61 days back to 38. Nothing clever.
A fixed reminder cadence and one owner per overdue account. Plus a hard
rule that nobody ships new work to a customer 60 days late.

We got the $212K back. Most of it.
