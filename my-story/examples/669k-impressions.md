these are the results we're seeing from a new client at 64stories, and the impressions keep climbing – driving cost of acquisition lower and lower

here's what we're doing:

we're just capturing stories of the company and getting the founder to share them in his voice

we have a Story Engineering process that makes everything run smoothly, but the secret to what we do is this:

we craft a really good story and narrative that aligns content and (more importantly) the company

the best companies in history do this well:

Salesforce had The End of Software
Shopify Armed the Rebels
Amazon communicated it's always Day 1
Canva said design was broken

and many more

they all row in unison, toward the same target. they aren't rowing in different directions, going in circles

when the CEO, founder, VP Marketing, and everyone else in a company shares this narrative, it makes buying from your company a lot easier. it makes funding and recruitment easier. and it makes your content easier.

here's our process:
1. Craft a great story and narrative
2. Create a process to capture stories inside and outside your company
3. Turn those story ideas into posts
4. Measure what works
5. Amplify your top performers with LinkedIn thought-leader ads
