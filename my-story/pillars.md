# Content pillars

For each pillar: a name, a one-sentence thesis, and a target share of your
output. Theses carry a belief, not a topic. "AI" is a topic; "founders who
run their own distribution will out-hire and out-sell founders who rent it"
is a thesis.

## 1. [Pillar name] (target NN%)

Thesis: [one sentence. What you are arguing, and what proof you bring.]

## 2. [Pillar name] (target NN%)

Thesis: [one sentence.]

## 3. [Pillar name] (target NN%)

Thesis: [one sentence.]

Rules of thumb: 3 pillars minimum or the voice reads scattered, 5 maximum or
none get depth. Targets are a starting split; adjust after your first measured
month with what-worked.
