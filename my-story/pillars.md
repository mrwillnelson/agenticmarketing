# Content pillars

## 1. Market while you sleep (target 30%)

Thesis: an agentic marketing system can run your distribution while you do the
work. Proof comes from running it: real target customer engagement numbers,
real pipeline, never a hypothetical.

## 2. The Agentic Marketing System (target 25%)

Thesis: the system itself, shown openly. How one recording becomes a week of
videos, articles, and posts. Give away the secrets, sell the implementation.

## 3. Agentic marketing (target 25%)

Thesis: founders and marketers can build and run AI agents that market.
Skills, taste, and judgment are the human's job, and they are the new shape
of the marketer's work.

## 4. AI tips (target 20%)

Thesis: small, immediately usable AI workflows a founder or marketer can run
this week. Each one saves hours and proves the bigger promise.

Targets above are Will's starting split; adjust yours after your first
measured month.
