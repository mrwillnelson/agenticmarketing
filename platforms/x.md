# X Constraints

## The fold

- A standard post is 280 characters. Every character earns its place; there is no fold to hide behind.
- In long-form posts, roughly the first 280 characters show before "Show more". They must work alone.
- The first line carries the scroll-stop, in every format. Never open with context, setup, or a summary of what follows.
- X renders plain text. No markdown, no bullets, no headers in posts or threads.
- Specifics over takes: a named number, a real moment, or a verbatim phrase beats an abstract claim.
- Links in the post cost reach. Include one only when the link is the point of the post.
- Line breaks are the only formatting tool. Use them to control pace, not to decorate.
- Capitalization and punctuation carry register on X. Match the account's approved posts; never mix registers in one post.
- No engagement bait in any format: no "wrong answers only", no quote-tweet farming, no reply-for-reach prompts.

## Threads

- Tweet 1 obeys the full hook law. It must stand alone as a post and earn the tap.
- One beat per tweet: one claim, one example, or one number. Never split a beat mid-sentence across tweets.
- Order beats as an arc, not a list. Each tweet creates the need for the next.
- Every tweet stays under 280 characters and reads complete on its own.
- No thread clichés: no thread emoji, no "a thread", no "1/12" length promises.
- Write each tweet to survive as a screenshot: a claim plus its support, never a fragment.
- Each tweet earns the next. If a beat can be cut without weakening the arc, cut the tweet.
- End on the strongest claim. No recap tweet, no follow request, no "if you enjoyed this".

## Long-form articles

- The title is the hook. Apply the same discipline as a post's first line; a descriptive title is a dead article.
- Hold one thesis for the whole piece. X rewards a single sharp argument, not a survey.
- Format sparser than LinkedIn: short paragraphs of 1 to 3 sentences with blank lines between them, and little else.
- Every paragraph advances the argument or carries proof. Cut anything that is setup for setup.
- End on the strongest plain sentence. No recap, no question, no pitch.
- Pair the article with a standalone post that hooks into it. The article does not distribute itself.
- No hashtags and no engagement-bait endings, in any format.
