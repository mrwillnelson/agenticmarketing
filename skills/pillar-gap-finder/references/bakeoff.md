### The capture plan (the calendar moment is invented for the bake-off)

The moment: Thursday's biweekly client idea call. Will's own 2026-03-12 post describes the practice verbatim: "Every two weeks I hop on a call with them to propose post ideas we believe will perform well." That call is capture practice happening live; nobody has ever seen it from the inside.

The plan, in four parts:

1. Moment: Thursday's biweekly idea call, first fifteen minutes.
2. The one question: "which post idea did we kill today, and what told us to kill it". A killed idea shows the judgment the pillar exists to demonstrate; the surviving ideas become client posts, not Will's.
3. Capture: record the call segment, with the client's permission, or take a two-line note the moment it happens.
4. Route: recording goes to `transcript-ideas` for scored candidates; anything unposted parks in the idea bank tagged capture-practice.

That is the whole output: one table, one plan, one honest "none parked".

## Why the gap table wins

The baseline invents demand ("your audience would love"), prescribes writing from air (a 5-tips carousel for a pillar with zero captured material behind it), and spreads effort across every pillar at once, which starves the starving one for another week. It also pushes volume at the crowding pillar's expense without ever counting anything. The with-skill output counts the gap (25 points, 4 weeks, score 100), names one pillar, checks the idea bank before asking for anything new, and books one real moment that produces the material. The carousel the baseline asked for would have violated the no-manufacture rule; the idea call it never noticed was the pillar's best source all along.
