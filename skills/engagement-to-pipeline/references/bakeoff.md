# Bake-off: spray vs routed

The same engager list handled two ways. The post is real: Will Nelson's storytelling post of 2026-02-26, 141 reactions and 35 comments. Key lines engagers responded to: "If you can't clearly explain what your company does, you probably don't have a clear strategy. The two problems are the same problem." "The gaps in your narrative reveal the gaps in your strategy." "Your head of sales tells one story, your head of product tells another." "Your story is your strategy, made legible."

The 12 engagers below are invented for this exercise. Assume `my-story/icp.md` says: founders and CEOs of B2B software companies, seed to Series B, post-revenue; VP Marketing is adjacent; never agencies, never vendors selling into founders.

## Baseline: the spray

The same DM to all 12, minutes after the post:

> "Hi {first name}, thanks for liking my post! I help founders turn their story into pipeline. Want to book a call this week?"

What happens: Priya and Noor wrote real comments and got a template that ignores them; the warmth their comments created is gone. Lena put her name on a reshare and got asked for a meeting in return. Tom, Greg, and Ricky, who are not buyers, got pitched anyway, which tells everyone watching the comments what engaging costs. Twelve messages, zero conversations, and the next post gets fewer comments because people learned the price of one.

## What changed, annotated

- Both DMs name the post and quote the person's own words. Neither could be sent to anyone else on the list, which is the test from hard rule 1.
- Both continue a conversation the recipient started, credit her phrasing over the post's, and end on a question about her company. Neither mentions 64stories, an offer, or a call.
- Reactions did not earn a DM, even at A tier. Marcus gets comments on his content first, so the eventual message has a real thread behind it.
- The repeat signals (Aisha, Jules) got acted on, because repetition outranks any single touch, but the action is engagement, not outreach.
- Tom, Greg, and Ricky are labeled honestly. The weekly note reports "2 conversations opened, 4 warming, 3 community" instead of "9 leads contacted".
- The follow-up policy is set before anyone goes quiet: if Priya does not answer, one nudge maximum, then she is a warm reader again, not a target.
