# Receipts: Will Nelson, Founder of 64stories.com

A real spread from Will's own LinkedIn posts, and what this skill would honestly do with it.

## The spread

Nine posts with captured engagement, all text format. Reaction counts across the sample, sorted:

- 3, 11, 12, 14, 15, 17, 51, 117, 141

The sample spans 2025-08-05 to 2026-03-25, wider than the 60-to-90-day window step 1 asks for; flag that too when reporting.

Median 15 reactions, so per hard rule 1 every post gets read as a ratio to 15, not as an absolute number. Three posts clear the 1.5x outlier line (51, 117, 141) and two sit under 0.5x (3 and 11). Per hard rule 3, any conclusion also has to survive deleting the 141 row.

The extremes:

- Top: 141 reactions, 35 comments (2026-02-26). Opener: "Ask a founder about storytelling and they'll point you to their marketing team." 195 words. Roughly 9.4x the sample median.
- Bottom: 3 reactions, 1 comment (2026-02-02). Opener: "If decisions stall when you're not in the room, this will feel uncomfortably familiar." 53 words. Roughly 0.2x the median.

## What the classification dimensions say

- Hook type: the winner is an audience-belief mirror, a specific behavior the reader has personally done. The loser addresses the reader directly but promises a feeling ("uncomfortably familiar") instead of showing anything concrete.
- Length band: the winner sits at the top of medium (195 words), the loser is short (53 words). One post per band proves nothing about bands.
- Pillar: both serve the same pillar, storytelling as strategy. Pillar does not separate them; the hook and the payload do.
- Format: both point readers to a longer article, and the whole sample is text. A constant explains no variance.

## The honest caveat

Nine posts clears this skill's 8-post floor to run at all, but it sits below the 3-posts-per-value threshold on almost every dimension value. Nine posts spread across the hook types leaves most values with one or two examples, so hard rule 2 applies: say "not enough data" instead of printing weights. The 141-versus-3 spread is therefore a lead, not a signal. It nominates audience-behavior-mirror openers as the named test for next month, with a count needed to reach 3 or more posts, and it bans nothing.
