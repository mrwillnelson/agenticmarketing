# Format anatomy

The vocabulary for deconstructing a post into a skeleton. Read this when a beat or engine is hard to name, or when a skeleton keeps coming out as a paraphrase of the source instead of an abstraction.

## The four parts of a skeleton

A complete skeleton names all four. If any part is missing, the remix will drift back toward copying the source to fill the gap.

1. Hook pattern: the move the first line makes, described so it could open a hundred different posts.
2. Beats in order: each structural unit and its job.
3. Rhythm profile: the physical shape of the post.
4. Engine: the psychological reason readers stay and react.

## Hook patterns

Name the move, never the words. Common patterns:

- Confession: admits a decision or belief the reader would hide.
- Status reversal: undoes something envied (fires the top performer, hands back the funding).
- Impossible number: a specific figure that should not coexist with the claim around it.
- Delayed admission: "it took me X to admit Y", where Y indicts the writer.
- Belief mirror: states what the audience privately suspects but has not said.
- Receipt open: leads with an artifact (the rejection email, the note from the debrief).
- Quiet contradiction: two true facts that cannot both be comfortable.

## Beat vocabulary

Name each paragraph or unit by its job. Working set:

- Confession: the admission that creates trust and stakes.
- Cost: what the situation was actually costing, made concrete.
- Evidence: the numbers or artifacts that prove the setup.
- Turn: the moment the writer's understanding flipped.
- Mechanism: how it actually works, the transferable part.
- Stakes raise: why this matters beyond the anecdote.
- Objection: the counterargument named and answered.
- Landing: the earned one-liner. Earned means the story paid for it; a landing pasted onto a different story reads as a fortune cookie.

A beat's job is portable. Its content never is.

## Rhythm profile

Record the shape, not the sentences:

- Paragraph sizes in order (e.g. 1 line, 2 lines, 2 lines, 1 line).
- Sentence length arc: where sentences run long and where they snap short. The shortest sentence usually sits on the confession or the landing; note where.
- Prose vs list, and if listed, how items are shaped.
- White space habits: one-line paragraphs, a lone sentence before the landing.

The rhythm profile is a default, not a law. The user's voice-model.md wins every conflict.

## Engine catalog

The engine is what the reader feels, not what the post says. A remix that matches topic but misses engine performs like a stranger's coat: technically it fits.

- Recognition: the reader sees their own unspoken situation named. Fuel: precise, ordinary detail.
- Status reversal: someone gives up or destroys a thing the audience covets, and is right to. Fuel: the sacrifice must be real and costly.
- Receipts: proof carries the post; the writer barely argues. Fuel: artifacts and numbers the reader can inspect.
- Permission: the post makes a shameful or unfashionable choice respectable. Fuel: the writer went first and shows the bill.
- Vicarious risk: the reader watches someone survive a mistake they fear making. Fuel: honest sequencing of the damage.
- Earned authority: competence demonstrated through specifics, never claimed. Fuel: decisions only an operator would know to make.
- Justice: a wrong named plainly, without performance. Fuel: restraint; outrage inflation kills it.

## Matching material to engine

In step 3 of the procedure, match on engine first and topic second. Tests for a genuine fit:

- Status reversal needs something the user actually gave up that others wanted. A mild preference is not a sacrifice.
- Receipts need artifacts the user really has. Reconstructed numbers fail the sniff test.
- Recognition needs a situation the user lived closely enough to supply the ordinary detail that triggers it.
- Permission needs the user to have paid the price already. Advice without a bill is a take, not a permission slip.

If every candidate story needs inflating to fit the engine, there is no fit. Refuse the remix and tell the user which engine their material does support.
