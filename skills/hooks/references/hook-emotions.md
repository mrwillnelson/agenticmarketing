# The five emotional lenses

Generate hook candidates through these lenses, two per lens, before selecting finalists. The lens names the feeling the reader has in the first second.

## Aha
A reframe that makes something confusing suddenly make sense. The reader learns why, not just what.
Signal to mine: hidden cause, mechanism behind a result, the variable everyone ignores.
Test: does the reader see an old problem in a new frame?

## Oh shit
A cost the reader did not know they were paying, or a risk already underway.
Signal to mine: costly mistake, silent failure, consequence discovered late.
Test: does the reader feel implicated, not just informed?

## WTF
A fact or outcome that violates the reader's model of how things work.
Signal to mine: specific number that should not be possible, surprising consequence, before-and-after with an absurd delta.
Test: is it true AND hard to believe? Invented absurdity is a hard fail.

## Hell yeah
Ambition and permission. The reader sees a move they want to make.
Signal to mine: sharp opinion, contrarian bet that paid off, outcome reversal.
Test: does the reader want to act, not just agree?

## Me too
Recognition. The reader has lived this exact moment and never seen it written down.
Signal to mine: confession, audience-belief mirror, small specific moment (a note, a call, a deleted draft).
Test: is the detail specific enough that agreement feels personal?

## Choosing among lenses

- Product and milestone content usually carries Aha or WTF best; hype pushes toward Hell yeah and fails.
- Personal narrative carries Me too and Oh shit.
- If two lenses produce equally strong candidates, pick the one the user's `my-story/hooks.md` history favors.
